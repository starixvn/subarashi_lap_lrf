General Info

  • The report lists out tickets (except running) of all Players on selected product.
  • Data is available from up to 3 years ago. Maximum search range is 1 month.
  • League and Match filters are only available for Sportsbook on a specific day.
  • To view tickets of a particular Player, enter system ID or external ID of that Player in "Username" filter
  • Select Base Currency to convert "Total" row in the report to the selected base currency.
  • When "Convert all figures to base currency" option is unchecked, all figures are displayed in original currencies (except the Total row).
  • Using "Live Status" filter to retrieve tickets that are Live or Non-Live. Note that Mix Live & Non-Live status is used for Parlay ticket.
  • "Status" filter has won, lost, draw are default. To query more data, please select other options in dropdown list.
    • {CO-productlist-section}
  • Cash Out: Enable when you choose the Sportsbook product is selected.
    • Include CO into Sportsbook: The Data area shall display Sportsbook including Cash Out tickets.
    • Exclude CO from Sportsbook: The Data area shall display Sportsbook excluding Cash Out tickets.
    • View CO only: The Product filter shall auto-select Sportsbook product and uncheck other products, Data get Cash Out tickets only.
    • {/CO-productlist-section}

Report Guidelines

  • External ID: The username registered by the ticket owner.
  • System ID: The Numeric sequential ID generated from the system for each account.
  • Ref No: A unique reference number given to each bet in the system.
  • Trans. Time: The time and date when the bet is accepted.
  • Choice: Shows detail information of Bet ticket such as K.O date and time of the match (only for Sportsbook), bet type, selected team, match and league name.
  • Odds: Displays the fixed odds value of each ticket.
  • Odds type: The odds format of each ticket.
  • Stake: The amount of money that Player has bet on each ticket.
  • Status: Shows the status of bet ticket.
  • Win Loss/Comm: Shows the player’s win loss and the commission given by Licensee.
  • Licensee WL/Comm: The total win loss amount Licensee has settled with Player including Comm.
  • Platform: The platform that Players used to place bets.
  • Live: Shows the ticket is Live or Non-Live.
