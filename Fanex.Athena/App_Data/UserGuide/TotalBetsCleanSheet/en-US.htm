General Info

  • This function lists all tickets which are placed on the team prevent the other team from scoring.
  • Clean Sheets means betting "Yes" on a team to keep a clean sheet (not to concede a goal) or "No" on a team not to keep a clean sheet (to concede a goal).

Search Filters

  • Currency: filters tickets placed in selected currency.
    • All: tickets of all supported currencies will be listed out.
  • Base Currency: Convert all data to base currency for reference only.

Report Guidelines

  • External ID: The username registered by the ticket owner.
  • System ID: The Numeric sequential ID generated from the system for each account.
  • Trans.Time: a unique reference number given to each bet ticket in the system including a timestamp of the time and date that the ticket was accepted.
  • Choice: show details of bet ticket such as customer choice, bet type, betting product, match and league.
  • Odds: odds value of ticket.
  • Stake: stake value of ticket.
  • Status: display status of ticket.
