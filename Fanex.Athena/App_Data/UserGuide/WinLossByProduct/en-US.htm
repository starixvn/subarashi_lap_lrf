General Info

  • This report contains only won/lose/draw tickets.
  • This report supports up-to-date data. You can filter data up to 3 months per query.
  • A red negative number indicates a loss for Licensee whereas the black positive indicates a win.
    • {casino-productlist-section}
  • Data of RNG Products, Live Casino Products will be delayed up to 5 minutes after settling tickets.
    • {/casino-productlist-section}

Search Filters

  • Report by: Data can be represented by Daily or Monthly.
  • Draw filter:
    • Normal: All won, lose and draw tickets will be included.
    • No Draws: Only won and lose tickets will be included.
    • No draws For Turnover Only: Draw tickets will not be counted in Turnover value.
  • Exclude Test Data: Excludes all tickets from test account.
  • Product: selects the Product type of bet tickets.
  • Currency: selects the currency which data will be retrieved.
    • All: all supported currencies will be listed out.
  • Base Currency: convert all data to based currency for reference only.

Report Guidelines

  • Currency: the list of selected currencies having bet ticket. To view in detail click on each currency.
  • Product: the products name which have been selected by yours.
  • Turnover: Customer turnover within a selected date range.
  • Total Transaction: total bet tickets of each selected products.
  • Customer W/L:
    • Win Loss: the amount customer won or lost based on their bets.
    • Comm: total commission counted on bet tickets.
    • Total: Total win/loss amount including commission.
      Total = Win/Loss + Commission
  • Licensee W/L: the win loss amount Licensee has settled with Customer.
    Licensee Win/Loss = – (Customer Win/Loss)
  • Comm: Licensee commission income for BA tickets and Racing Partner.
  • Amount Due: the settlement amount that Licensee has paid or received for each Product.
    Note that: for Amount Due, the black negative number indicates the Licensee receiving amount and black positive number indicates the Licensee paying amount.
    • For (Sportsbook, Number Game, Virtual Sports) products, Amount Due is the settlement amount between Licensee and Company.
    • For BA, Amount Due is the settlement amount between Licensee and BA service.
