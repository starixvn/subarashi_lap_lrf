General Info

  • A commission group enables licensee to set up commission percentages of specific products for a group of customers. When customer places bet, he could receive commission based on commission rates of his group. However, BA customers will not receive commission.
  • This function supports licensee to monitor his customer commission group.

Report Guidelines

  • New Group: create a new commission group.
    • Step 1: Click "New Group" button.
    • Step 2: After inputting group name, check whether defined name is available by clicking "Check availability" button.
    • Step 3: Define licensee's prefer commission settings.
      Note: Customer commission amount = Commission (%) * Customer stake
    • Step 4: Check on checkbox "Set as default group" to set that group as default group. Note that, all newly registered customers will be automatically assigned to this default group. There will be no change to existing customers.
    • Step 5: Select a color for new group.
    • Step 6: Click "Submit" to create defined commission group.
  • Group Name: click view/edit settings of selected commission group.
  • No. of Customer: number of customers in selected commission group. Click to view Customer List.
  • : edit selected commission group. The new commission settings will affect to all customers in that group immediately.
  • : delete selected commission group.
    • Keep current commission: delete the group, but still keep current commission rates of that group’s customers.
    • Clear customer’s commission: delete the group and clear all commission rates of that group’s customers.
