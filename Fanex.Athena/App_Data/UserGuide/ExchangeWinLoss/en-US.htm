General Info

  • This report supports up-to-date data. You can filter data up to 1 month per query.
  • The data may have a delay of around 20 minutes for Settled tickets and 65 minutes for Resettled tickets.
  • A red negative number indicates a loss for Licensee whereas the black positive indicates a win.
  • OANDA Exchange Service will use the exchange rate of the end date of each selected month when converting the figures.

Search Filters

  • Currency: Select the currency which data will be retrieved.
    • All: All supported currencies will be listed out.
  • Base Currency: Convert all data to base currency for reference only.
  • Username: Enter the external ID or system ID of the searched account.
    • Note that, if you have not specified any Player account then the Report will list out data of all Players within the selected date range.
  • Exclude Test Data: Excludes all tickets from test account.
  • View Resettled Ticket Only: Gets Resettled tickets only within the selected date range.
  • View in Settled and Resettled Date: Gets data based on both Settled Date & Resettled Date following the selected date range.
  • View in Settled Date: Gets data of the first-time settled tickets based on the selected date range.

Report Guidelines

  • Date: can be daily or monthly based on your selection and its format is (mm/dd/yyyy).
  • Currency: shall display based on your selection.
  • Turnover: Total Player turnover within selected filters.
  • Net Turnover: The amount of turnover after deducting draw tickets.
    • Net Turnover = Turnover - the total turnover of draw tickets.
  • Total Transaction: Total bet tickets within selected filters.
  • Player:
    • Player W/L: The amount player won or lost based on their bets.
    • Player Comm: The commission amount that players have paid to you based on won tickets.
    • Total: Total win/loss amount including commission.
      Total = Win/Loss + Commission
  • Licensee:
    • Licensee W/L: The win loss amount Licensee has settled with Player.
      Licensee W/L = – (Player W/L)
    • Amount Due To: The settlement amount that you have received or paid to Company.
      • Note that for Amount Due To, the negative number indicates the receiving amount from Company and the positive number indicates the paying amount to Company.
    • Total: Net win/loss amount after sharing with Company.
      Total = Licensee WL - Player Comm - Amount Due To
