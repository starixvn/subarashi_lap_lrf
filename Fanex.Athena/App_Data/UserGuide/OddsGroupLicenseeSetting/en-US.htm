General Info

  • This feature enables Licensees to independently update their own odds group across their Currencies.

Search Filters

    {subsidiary-section-2}
  • Subsidiary: Select the subsidiaries for which you want to change the odds group.
  • Currency: Select the currencies for which you want to change the odds group.
  • Subsidiary Search:
    • Checked: Shows the Subsidiary column, allowing you to change the odds group for specific Subsidiaries.
    • Unchecked: Hides the Subsidiary column, allowing you to change the odds group only for specific Currencies.
    • {/subsidiary-section-2} {no-subsidiary-section-2}
  • Currency: Select the currencies for which you want to change the odds group.
    • {/no-subsidiary-section-2}

Updating Guidelines

    {subsidiary-section-3}
  • Step 1: Change the odds group in the row of the Subsidiary or Currency that you want to update.
    • {/subsidiary-section-3} {no-subsidiary-section-3}
  • Step 1: Change the odds group in the row of the Currency that you want to update.
    • {/no-subsidiary-section-3}
  • Step 2: Click "Update" button.
  • Step 3: Click "OK" in the confirmation popup. If you click "Cancel", no changes will be made.
  • Step 4: View Result.
    • Update Successful: you will see "Update Odds Group Setting succesfully." in the Status column.
    • Update Fail: you will see "Sorry, an error occured while processing your request." in the Status column.
