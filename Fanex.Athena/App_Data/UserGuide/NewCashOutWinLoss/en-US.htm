General Info

  • The report contains only won/lost/draw for Cash Out tickets.
  • This report supports up-to-date data. You can filter data up to 3 months per query.
  • A red negative number indicates a loss for Licensee whereas the black positive indicates a win.
  • OANDA Exchange Service will use the exchange rate of the end date of each selected month when converting the figures.

Search Filters

  • Report by: Data can be represented by Daily or Monthly.
  • Exclude Test Data: Excludes all tickets from the test account.
  • Currency: Selects the currency which data will be retrieved.
    • All: All supported currencies of will be listed out.
  • Base Currency: Convert all data to base currency for reference only.
  • Convert all figures to Base Currency: Convert all data into base currency. Otherwise, data of currency row will be in original currency and data of Total rows will be always converted into base currency.

Report Guidelines

  • Date: Can be daily or monthly based on your selection and its format is (mm/dd/yyyy).
  • Sport: All sports are offered Cash Out will be displayed in the dropdown list.
  • Currency: The offered currency has been selected by yours. To view in detail click on each currency.
  • Turnover: Total customer turnover of all bets within a selected date range.
  • Total Transaction: Total bet tickets within a selected date range.
  • Customer W/L:
    • Win Loss: The amount customer won or lost based on their bets.
    • Comm: Total commission counted won, lost, and draw tickets.
    • Total: Total win/loss amount including commission.
      Total = Win/Loss + Commission
  • Licensee W/L: The win loss amount Licensee has settled with Customer.
    Licensee Win/Loss = – (Customer Win/Loss)
  • BA Commission: Licensee Commission income for BA tickets (including won and lost tickets).
  • Amount Due To BA: The settlement amounts between Licensee and BA service.
    Note that for Amount Due to BA, the black negative number indicates the receiving amount from BA and the black positive number indicates the paying amount to BA.
