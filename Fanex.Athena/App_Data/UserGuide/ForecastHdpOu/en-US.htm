General Info

  • This total bets report shows users a forecast for their potential win/loss amount in a match based on different results.

Search Filters

  • Currency: filters tickets placed in selected currency.
    • All: tickets of all supported currencies will be listed out.
  • Base Currency: Convert all data to base currency for reference only.

Report Guidelines

  • Handicap: forecast of handicap tickets.
    • A positive number (e.g. 10) indicates Licensee will lose more than win.
    • A negative number (e.g. –10) indicates Licensee will win more than lose.
  • Over/Under: forecast of over/under tickets.
  • :view forecast and bet list of selected match.
