Search Filters

  • Currency: selects the currency which data will be retrieved.
    • All: all supported currencies will be listed out.
  • Base Currency: convert all data to based currency for reference only.
  • Exclude Test Data: Excludes all tickets from test account.
  • Draw filter:
    • Normal: All won, lose and draw tickets will be included.
    • No Draws: Only won and lose tickets will be included.
    • No draws For Turnover Only: Draw tickets will not be counted in Turnover value.
  • Platform: filters data by Asia/New Asia/China/Euro platform, SmartSite, WAP and Tablet.
    • Asia/New Asia/China/Euro: includes bets from Asia/New Asia/China/Euro platform.
    • WAP: includes bets from WAP.
    • Smartsite: includes bets from Smart Site.
    • Tablet: includes bets from Tablet.
  • Product: selects the Product type of bet tickets.

Report Guidelines

  • External ID: the username registered by ticket owner.
  • System ID: the Numeric sequential ID generated from system for each account.
    Click on External ID or System ID to view bet list of that member.
  • Currency: offered currency has been selected by user.
  • Turnover: Customer turnover within the selected date range.
  • Total Transaction: total bet tickets of selected products.
  • Customer W/L:
    • Win Loss: the amount customer won or lost based on their bets.
    • Comm: total amount of bonus counted on bet tickets.
    • Total: Total win/loss amount including commission.
      Total = Win/Loss + Commission
  • Licensee W/L: the win loss amount Licensee has settled with Customer.
    Licensee Win/Loss = – (Customer Win/Loss)
